# Doom Scroll Monitor: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Doom Scroll Monitor is an Android app that reminds you to take a break when you
have been scrolling social media apps for longer than you wanted.

## The short version

- Everything the app knows stays on your phone.
- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What the app uses and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Usage access** (which app is on screen) | To count how long you spend in the apps you chose to watch, such as Instagram or YouTube, and to show your daily times and the last 7 days on the stats screen. The app only checks which app is in the foreground and when. It does not see what you view, type or post inside those apps. | Read on your phone: kept in memory while monitoring runs, and added up from Android's own usage history when you open your stats. The times are not saved or sent anywhere. |
| **Display over other apps** | To show the break reminder on top of the app you are scrolling. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Notifications** | To show that monitoring is on, and to show the reminder if the pop-up can't be displayed. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Start at boot** | To turn monitoring back on after your phone restarts, if you had it on. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Calendar** (optional, off unless you turn it on) | If you choose "Today's calendar" for the reminder, the app reads the rest of today's events (start time and title) from your phone's own calendar and lists the next three in the reminder. It asks for calendar access only when you choose this. | Read on your phone at the moment the reminder appears. It is not saved, backed up or sent anywhere. |
| **Your settings and to-do list** | Your reminder limit, your daily goal, the apps you chose to watch, what the reminder shows (to-dos or calendar), and the "things I'd rather be doing" list you type in. | Saved in the app's private storage on your phone. |
| **Your streak** | To show how many days in a row you stayed within your daily goal. The app saves only three things: your current streak, your best streak, and the last day it counted. It does not keep a history of your times. | Saved in the app's private storage on your phone. |
| **Small choices for this phone** | For example, that you tapped "Got it" to hide a tip. | Saved in the app's private storage on your phone. |

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your phone:

- **Your settings and to-do list** may be included in your device backup (for
  example, Google's backup service) and in a direct transfer to a new phone, so they
  come back when you set up a new phone.
- **Your streak** is never included in cloud backups. It only comes along when you
  move to a new phone with a direct phone-to-phone transfer (cable or local
  wireless), which doesn't go through any server.
- **Your usage times, calendar events and the small choices for this phone** are
  never included in either.

You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

## Sharing

The app does not share, sell or send any data to the developer or to anyone else.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. You can also clear it from
**Settings → Apps → Doom Scroll Monitor → Storage → Clear storage**.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Changes

If a future version reads anything new, this policy will be updated before that
version is released and will say exactly what is read and why. The app will still
keep everything on your phone.
