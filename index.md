# Doom Scroll Monitor: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Doom Scroll Monitor is an Android app that reminds you to take a break when you
have been scrolling social media apps for longer than you wanted.

## The short version

- Everything the app knows stays on your phone.
- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What the app uses and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Usage access** (which app is on screen) | To count how long you spend in the apps you chose to watch, such as Instagram or YouTube. The app only checks which app is in the foreground. It does not see what you view, type or post inside those apps. | Read on your phone and kept in memory while monitoring runs. It is not saved or sent anywhere. |
| **Display over other apps** | To show the break reminder on top of the app you are scrolling. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Notifications** | To show that monitoring is on, and to show the reminder if the pop-up can't be displayed. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Start at boot** | To turn monitoring back on after your phone restarts, if you had it on. | Nothing is collected. |
| **Your settings and to-do list** | Your reminder limit, the apps you chose to watch, and the "things I'd rather be doing" list you type in. | Saved in the app's private storage on your phone. |

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's
settings and to-do list in your device backup (for example, Google's backup
service) so they come back when you set up a new phone. Your usage information is
never included. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

## Sharing

The app does not share, sell or send any data to the developer or to anyone else.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. You can also clear it from
**Settings → Apps → Doom Scroll Monitor → Storage → Clear storage**.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Changes

If a future version reads anything new (for example your phone's own calendar, to
show today's events in the reminder), this policy will be updated before that
version is released and will say exactly what is read and why. The app will still
keep everything on your phone.
